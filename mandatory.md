# Mandatory Impressum Fields

*Legal basis: DDG § 5, MStV § 18 Abs. 2, JMStV § 7, VSBG, § 44b UrhG*
*This is a reference guide, not legal advice.*

---

## Who Needs an Impressum?

All commercially operated "Telemedien" (telemedia) require an Impressum. This includes:
- Blogs, websites, mobile and web apps
- Social media profiles and company pages (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Xing, YouTube, TikTok, etc.)

Private accounts are exempt only if both conditions apply:
- Not directed at the general public, AND
- No commercial connection (no advertising, affiliate programs, influencer partnerships)

Employee and influencer profiles are considered commercial if used to communicate with business customers, share employer promotions, or act as brand ambassadors.

---

## 1. Basic Identity (Grundangaben)

- Business type: Freiberufler, Unternehmen, or other commercial activity
- Full legal name of owner or company (all given names spelled out; include full legal form, e.g. "Mustermann UG (haftungsbeschränkt)", "Peter Müller und Steve Schneider GbR", "Freunde der Zukunft e.V.")
- Street address — no PO box
- Country of operation

---

## 2. Contact (Kontaktmöglichkeiten)

- **Email address** — must enable rapid electronic contact (DDG § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 2); monitored regularly during business hours
- **Phone number** — mandatory for consumer-facing businesses; alternatively a contact form URL or website chat is accepted if responses are prompt
- **Fax** *(optional since 28.05)*
- **Contact form URL** *(optional, unless used as substitute for phone)*
- **Weitere Kontaktmöglichkeiten** *(optional)* — messenger services that allow correspondence saved on a permanent data carrier (e.g. WhatsApp Business number); list platform and contact detail

---

## 3. Authorized Representatives (Vertretungsberechtigte)

Required for companies, associations, and authorities:

- Full name(s) and function(s) of authorized representative(s) (e.g. "Max Mustermann (Geschäftsführer)")
- Note: sole traders (Einzelunternehmen) must use "Inhaber", not "Geschäftsführer"
- If another legal entity is the authorized representative, the chain must be traced to a natural person (e.g. "Komplementär XYZ GmbH ... Vertretungsberechtigt: Max Müller (Geschäftsführer)")

---

## 4. Business Information (Angaben zum Unternehmen)

- **Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (USt-ID)** — only if assigned; format DE + 9 digits. Required for cross-border EU business.
- **Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer (W-IdNr)** — only if assigned. Not the same as personal tax ID (IdNr) or Steuernummer (~99/1234/12345) — those must NOT be disclosed.
- **Geschäftsbereich** — business area; required in Austria, optional in Germany
- **AGB link** *(optional)* — URL to general terms and conditions if published online

---

## 5. Register Entry (Registereintrag)

Required if the entity is legally registered (DDG § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 4):

- Register type:
  - Handelsregister
  - Vereinsregister
  - Partnerschaftsregister
  - Genossenschaftsregister
  - Unternehmensregister
  - Firmenbuch (Österreich)
- Name of the register / Registergericht (e.g. "Amtsgericht Beispielsstadt")
- Registration number (e.g. "HRB 1234")

---

## 6. Licensed / Regulated Activities (Aufsichtsbehörde)

Required if the business needs a governmental or state license (DDG § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 3):

Examples: Gaststätten, Inkassobüros, Taxiunternehmen, regulated professions (doctors, lawyers, pharmacists)

- Name and full address of the competent supervisory authority (Aufsichtsbehörde)
- Website URL of the supervisory authority *(optional but recommended)*

Note: The supervisory authority is not always the same body that issued the licence — confirm with the licensing body.

---

## 7. Regulated Professions (Reglementierte Berufe)

Required for regulated professions (EU-Richtlinie 2005/36/EG):

Covered professions include: Altenpfleger/in, Apotheker/in, Architekt/in, Arzt/Ärztin, Erzieher/in, Gesundheits- und Krankenpfleger/in, Heilpraktiker/in, Innenarchitekt/in, Kraftfahrzeugsachverständige/r, Landschaftsarchitekt/in, Notar/in, Physiotherapeut/in, Rechtsanwalt/Rechtsanwältin, Steuerberater/in, Wirtschaftsprüfer/in, Zahnarzt/Zahnärztin, and others.

- **Gesetzliche Berufsbezeichnung** — legal professional title (preset list or free text)
- **Country where the title was granted** — Deutschland, Österreich, Schweiz, Frankreich, Niederlande, Spanien, Polen, Großbritannien, or other
- **Kammer membership** (if applicable):
  - Name and address of the chamber or comparable professional body (e.g. Rechtsanwaltskammer, Architektenkammer, Ärztekammer)
  - Website/contact URL of the chamber *(optional but recommended)*
- **Berufsrechtliche Regelungen** — applicable professional regulations and URL where they can be accessed (note: state-level regulations may also apply, e.g. Berufsordnung für die bayerischen Zahnärzte)

---

## 8. Professional Liability Insurance (Berufshaftpflichtversicherung)

Required in the EU if a professional liability insurance has been taken out (Art. 2 Richtlinie 2006/123/EG; DE: § 2 Abs. 2 DL-InfoV):

- Name and full address of the insurance company
- Geographic scope of coverage (e.g. Deutschland, EU, Weltweit)

Note: Mandatory for some professions (architects, doctors, lawyers). Optional for others.

---

## 9. Editorial / Journalistic Content (MStV § 18)

Required if the online presence provides regular, public-facing reporting or commentary (blogs, news sites, Facebook pages with regular news-style posts):

- Full name and address of the person responsible for content (Verantwortlicher i.S.d. § 18 Abs. 2 MStV)
- If same address as operator: "(Adresse wie oben)" is sufficient
- Multiple responsible persons allowed, e.g. "Tony Stark (Ressort Technik), Peter Parker (Ressort Naturwissenschaften)"

---

## 11. Youth Protection Officer (Jugendschutzbeauftragte/r)

Required under JMStV § 7 if the offering is publicly accessible AND contains content that could impair the development of children or youth (not limited to explicit content — includes violence-glorifying content, forums where such content may appear, etc.).

Exemption: fewer than 50 employees OR demonstrably fewer than 10 million monthly visits on average — if the operator joins a voluntary self-regulation body (freiwillige Selbstkontrolle).

- At minimum: email address (Kontaktmöglichkeit) of the Jugendschutzbeauftragte/r

## 12. Consumer Dispute Resolution (Verbraucherstreitbeilegung)

Disclosure is required if ALL four criteria apply:
1. Established in the EU
2. Offers goods or services to consumers
3. More than 10 employees (headcount) at end of prior year
4. Has an online presence (website, e-shop, Amazon seller page, social media page) or AGB

Note: Having 10+ employees means you must disclose your stance — you are not automatically obligated to participate.

**Select and include one of the following statements:**

No participation:
> Wir sind nicht bereit und nicht verpflichtet an einem Streitbeilegungsverfahren vor einer Verbraucherstreitschlichtungsstelle teilzunehmen.

Voluntary participation:
> Wir sind zur Beilegung von Streitigkeiten mit Verbrauchern vor einer Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle bereit.

Mandatory participation:
> Wir sind zur Beilegung von Streitigkeiten mit Verbrauchern vor einer Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle verpflichtet.

If participating (voluntary or mandatory):
- Name and postal address of the Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle
- Website URL of the Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle

Also recommended: include the same notice in your AGB.

---

## 13. Social Media Presence

Required if social media profiles link to this Impressum and the profile name differs from the operator name (or if employees/managers link their personal profiles):

- List all platforms and profile URLs covered by this Impressum
- Example: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn profile URLs

---

## 14. Accessibility Requirement (DDG § 5 opening clause)

The Impressum must be:
- **Leicht erkennbar** — easily recognizable (e.g. labelled "Impressum")
- **Unmittelbar erreichbar** — immediately accessible, typically via a direct footer link; maximum two clicks from any page
- **Ständig verfügbar** — permanently available, not behind a login

---

## Optional Additions

- **Bildnachweise** — image credits (attribution for photos/illustrations used)
- **AGB link** — link to general terms and conditions
- **AI training opt-out (Nutzungsvorbehalt)** — generated text:
  > Der Inhaber dieser Website gestattet die Nutzung oder das Herunterladen von Inhalten dieser Website durch Dritte für die Entwicklung, das Training oder den Betrieb von künstlicher Intelligenz oder anderen maschinellen Lernsystemen ("Text und Data Mining") ausschließlich mit ausdrücklicher schriftlicher Zustimmung des Inhabers. Ohne eine solche Zustimmung ist es untersagt, die Inhalte für Text und Data Mining zu verwenden.
  
  Additionally recommended: `<meta name="robots" content="noai, noimageai">` in the HTML head, and AI bot entries in robots.txt.

- **Haftungsausschluss** — liability disclaimer for own content and linked external sites
- **Urheberrechte und Markenrechte** — copyright and trademark notice
- **Hinweis auf Rechtsverstöße** — request for notification of legal violations
- **Freitext** — free text fields at top and bottom of Impressum
